Warranty and Returns

1. Product Warranty

All products purchased in the online store pristavkigamleng.com are covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

If you find a defect in the purchased product, please contact us immediately through the Contact page. We will review your claim and provide instructions for resolving the issue as quickly as possible.

2. Returns Policy

The customer has the right to return the purchased goods within 14 days of receiving the order, without providing a reason, in accordance with consumer protection laws.

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team through the Contact page.
  • Provide your order details and specify the reason for the return (optional).
  • Ensure the product is in its original condition, unused, and with all packaging intact.
  • Ship the item to the address provided by our support team.

Refunds will be processed within 14 days of receiving the returned item. Shipping costs for returns may be the responsibility of the customer unless the item is defective or damaged.

3. Contact

If you have any questions about our warranty and returns policy, please contact us. We are here to assist you!
