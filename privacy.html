Privacy Policy

1. General Information

This privacy policy outlines the rules for processing personal data of users using the website pristavkigamleng.com. Protecting the privacy of our customers is our priority, which is why we collect and process personal data in accordance with applicable laws, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. Data Collected

When placing an order or registering on our website, we may ask you to provide the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address

This information is necessary for fulfilling orders and contacting customers in case of questions or issues.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

We process users' personal data for the following purposes:

  • Fulfilling orders placed by users
  • Contacting customers regarding their orders
  • Providing information about new products, promotions, and special offers if the user has consented to receive newsletters
  • Improving the functionality of the website and analyzing traffic

4. Data Protection

All personal data is protected using appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure its security against unauthorized access, modification, or destruction. Only authorized personnel have access to the data, and they are required to maintain confidentiality.

5. User Rights

Every user has the right to:

  • Accessing their personal data
  • Correcting, deleting, or limiting the processing of data
  • Withdrawing consent for data processing at any time
  • Transferring data to another data controller
  • Filing a complaint with a supervisory authority in case of a violation of data protection regulations

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve the quality of the services provided and to tailor content to the user's individual preferences. Users can manage their cookie settings through their web browser. Detailed information on this topic can be found in our Cookie Policy.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this privacy policy. All changes will be posted on this page. We recommend regularly reviewing the policy to stay informed of the latest data protection rules.

8. Contact

For any questions regarding the privacy policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
